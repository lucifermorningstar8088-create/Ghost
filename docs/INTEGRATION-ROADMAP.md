# Ghost Skill Integration Roadmap

## Wave 1: Trust and reliability

Wire orchestration, account isolation, authorization gates, evidence ledger, bounded recovery, security monitoring, and independent quality checks.

## Wave 2: Channel intelligence

Connect approved metadata and analytics sources; verify ingestion, freshness, channel audits, research provenance, and experiments.

## Wave 3: Content excellence

Connect rights-cleared assets, creative review, media production adapters, packaging, captions, and final-media checks.

## Wave 4: Controlled autonomy

Enable publishing only after OAuth scopes, platform API limits, idempotency, preflight, approval policy, read-back, and recovery are tested.

## Wave 5: Revenue and optimization

Add sponsor CRM, eligibility tracking, actual-versus-estimated revenue, cost controls, and experiment-driven optimization. Do not promise virality, platform approval, or revenue.
